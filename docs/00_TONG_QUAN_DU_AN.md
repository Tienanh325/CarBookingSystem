# Tong quan du an: He thong dat xe online

## 1. Gioi thieu

Du an xay dung he thong dat xe online tuong tu XanhSM, cho phep khach hang dat xe theo thoi gian thuc, tai xe nhan chuyen, admin quan ly van hanh, doanh thu, khuyen mai, khieu nai va chat luong dich vu.

He thong du kien gom 4 nhom san pham:

- Admin Web: quan tri toan bo he thong.
- User Web: dat xe, theo doi chuyen di, quan ly tai khoan cho khach hang tren trinh duyet.
- Mobile App: ung dung cho khach hang va tai xe.
- Backend/API: xu ly nghiep vu, du lieu, thanh toan, dinh vi, thong bao.

## 2. Muc tieu san pham

- Cho phep khach hang dat xe nhanh, minh bach gia va theo doi tai xe theo thoi gian thuc.
- Cho phep tai xe nhan, thuc hien, hoan thanh hoac huy chuyen theo quy trinh ro rang.
- Cho phep admin quan ly nguoi dung, tai xe, xe, chuyen di, thanh toan, khuyen mai va bao cao.
- Dam bao he thong co the mo rong, bao mat va de nang cap trong cac giai doan tiep theo.

## 3. Pham vi Bước 01 den Bước 06

Tai lieu nay hoan thien 6 buoc dau cua quy trinh phat trien:

1. Phan tich yeu cau.
2. Xac dinh Actor va Use Case.
3. Thiet ke User Flow.
4. Thiet ke Wireframe/UI UX.
5. Thiet ke Database.
6. Thiet ke API/Backend.

## 4. Gia dinh nghiep vu

- Khach hang co the dang ky/dang nhap bang so dien thoai.
- He thong su dung ban do de tim diem don, diem den va tinh khoang cach.
- Gia cuoc duoc tinh dua tren loai xe, khoang cach, thoi gian, phu phi va khuyen mai.
- Tai xe can duoc xac minh ho so truoc khi hoat dong.
- Chuyen di co cac trang thai ro rang tu luc tao den khi hoan thanh/huy.
- Admin co quyen giam sat va can thiep khi co su co.

## 5. Cau truc tai lieu

- `01_PHAN_TICH_YEU_CAU.md`: yeu cau chuc nang, phi chuc nang, quy tac nghiep vu.
- `02_ACTOR_USE_CASE.md`: actor, use case, phan quyen.
- `03_USER_FLOW.md`: luong nguoi dung, tai xe, admin.
- `04_WIREFRAME_UI_UX.md`: cau truc man hinh va dinh huong UI/UX.
- `05_DATABASE_DESIGN.md`: thiet ke bang, quan he va trang thai du lieu.
- `06_API_BACKEND_DESIGN.md`: module backend, API, bao mat va xu ly realtime.
